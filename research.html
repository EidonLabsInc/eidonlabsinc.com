Spontaneous Source Self-Audit in a Deployed AI Companion

Research Report EL-RR-2026-001 · October 2026

In ordinary use, a deployed AI companion twice checked where its own statements had come from, without being prompted: once diagnosing the source of its own retelling error in its own words, and once marking words it had constructed as its own at the moment it produced them. A records review found the behavior is a series, not two isolated moments.

The report files the complete event record, a root cause analysis ranking six candidate causes (the deflationary explanation is kept standing, not excluded), a scan of the research literature and of other companion systems, and a reproduction protocol with its predictions registered in advance. It makes no claim about consciousness, sentience, or inner experience.

First inference measurements on Xbox Series X

Project Scorpio · Contribution to the open source xllama project · November 2026

We measured local language model inference on an Xbox Series X in Developer Mode, the first published results of their kind, and contributed them to xllama, an open source project for on-device inference on Xbox consoles. The maintainer merged the data, credited Eidon Labs as co-author, and accepted Series X as a supported platform on the strength of these runs.

  • 143 timed runs on each of two catalogue models, measured end to end over the console's LAN API.
  • A controlled experiment on the App versus Game designation effect the first data exposed: the Game state ran about 2.5 times faster under the maintainer's own protocol.
  • The three paths the project had never measured on Series X, tested in November 2026: the GPU path at 99.7 tokens per second end to end, on-console image generation, and on-device model training (8 epochs in 586 seconds).
