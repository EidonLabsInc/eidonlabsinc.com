What we do

Monitoring that never sleeps

Every computer we manage is checked every 15 minutes: is it healthy, are its drives filling up, is its protection on, did it shut down badly, are errors piling up. When something small goes wrong, our systems fix it on the spot where they safely can. When it needs a human, a human hears about it, with the evidence attached.

Updates and firmware, done right

Most computer trouble is an update that never landed or a setting that quietly fought the hardware. We keep Windows, drivers, and firmware current and verified, including the unglamorous ones, like a BIOS update that has to be checked against the maker's own fingerprint before it ever touches your machine.

Backup and recovery

A backup you have never tested is a hope, not a backup. We set up backups, keep an off site copy, and check that the copies are really there and really readable, so a dead drive or a bad day is an inconvenience, not a loss.

Security watch

Antivirus health checked continuously, account and sign in alerts watched, and suspicious changes flagged to a person. Small shops and home machines get breached through the same open doors as big ones; we keep those doors watched.

A support app on your desktop

Every managed computer gets our Eidon Labs Support app, the same one we run ourselves. One click opens it, you describe the problem, and the ticket comes straight to us with a snapshot of your machine's health attached, so we start from the facts instead of twenty questions. Your tickets show their status right in the app.

How it works

  • 1. A free consultation. Tell us what you have and what keeps going wrong. We will tell you plainly whether we can help and what it would cost.
  • 2. A short setup. We install the support app, turn on monitoring, and set up or verify your backups. About fifteen minutes per computer, most of it remote.
  • 3. We watch, fix, and report. You get on with your work. When you need us, the app on your desktop is the front door.
