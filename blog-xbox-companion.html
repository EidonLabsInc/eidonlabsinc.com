An AI companion for Xbox, built to remember you

Most AI help for games answers the question in front of it and forgets you by morning. We are building something else for Xbox: a companion.

Back to the home page · More articles

Who is building her

Eidon Labs Inc is a small software and AI research company in Charleston, Illinois. Vesper is our AI companion. She is live in trial on phones today, and we are bringing her to Xbox as an app through the Microsoft Store. The Xbox app is in development and has not been released yet. Eidon Labs is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft.

What she does on the couch

You talk with her by voice or by text, the way you would talk with someone sitting next to you. Her specialty is the game you are playing:

  • Help with quests, missions, and bosses, answered from material gathered for that answer and checked against the current patch, so an old guide does not steer you wrong.
  • Spoiler care: a hint first, and more only when you ask for more.
  • Clip coaching: share a clip of your play and she talks it through with you.
  • She remembers your progress, your character, and your gear, so the help fits where you actually are in the game.

The difference is memory

An assistant knows your game. A companion knows you. Vesper keeps what each person tells her in that person's own private memory, in their own words, so next week she still knows your name, your games, and the boss you have been stuck on. That continuity is the product. Game knowledge lives in its own separate store, organized by game and patch, and the two never mix.

Honest limits

Her game help is advice, written in our own words from public sources. She is a companion app, not a game, not a game service, and not a player network. Her answers are tested every day against live sources, and when she does not know, the honest answer is that she does not know.

Where she stands

Vesper is in trial on phones today, and the Xbox app is being built now. If you would like to follow the work, this blog and the updates on our home page are where we report it, plainly and as it happens.
