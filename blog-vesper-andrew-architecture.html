We are integrating Andrew's proven architecture into Vesper.

Andrew was designed from the start to remember truly. Every claim he makes about a conversation is checked against the written record. Facts are kept separate from guesses. Nothing gets filed as true that was never said. He works, and he rarely gets his own history wrong.

Now Vesper, our gaming companion, is getting that same foundation. Her memory is being rebuilt into typed stores: facts as facts, inferences labeled and set apart, open questions kept as questions. A gate checks her claims in both directions. She cannot claim something happened without the record in hand, and she cannot deny something happened until the full record has been searched.

The first three phases are built and have passed our internal review. The migration phase is underway today.

One architecture. Two very different minds. One standard: if it is not in the record, it does not get said as fact.
