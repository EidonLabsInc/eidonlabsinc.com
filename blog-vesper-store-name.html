Vesper is coming to the Microsoft Store

We have reserved her Store name, VesperAi, in Microsoft Partner Center under our publisher account, Eidon Labs Inc. It is the next step on the road to bringing our AI companion to Windows and Xbox.

Back to the home page · More articles

Vesper is our AI companion: a conversational partner with her own memory, her own library, and a specialty in helping players with the games they are playing. She is in trial on phones today, by invitation, and her Xbox version is in development. Today that version has a name on the Microsoft Store road: the product VesperAi now exists in Partner Center under our publisher account, in draft, with its identity issued.

To be plain about what this is and is not: a name reservation is a beginning, not a launch. The Store listing is not public yet, nothing is for sale, and the product has its certification road still to walk. What changed today is that the road is open and the first gate on it is behind us. The planned shape has not changed: subscriptions through the Microsoft Store at $4.99 a month for chat and $9.99 a month for chat plus game assist, with a one week free trial of the full version, when the Xbox version opens.

Meanwhile the trial keeps running, and this week it gained its members shelf: the complete data packs behind our public reports, starting with the full Project Scorpio measurement record, which trial members can now download from the Reports Library with their token. Her measured state, including the faults we have not fixed yet, stands on her product page, updated today.

Thank you to everyone who has followed the build so far. The Store road is open, and she is walking it.
