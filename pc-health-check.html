What the check covers

Drive and hardware health

Hard drive and SSD health, the early warning signs of a failing disk, memory, and whether your machine has been shutting down unexpectedly, and why.

Restart and boot faults

Computers that will not restart, hang on updates, or take forever to start usually leave the reason written in their own logs. We read them and tell you what they say.

Security and updates

Whether your antivirus is on and current, whether Windows updates are landing or quietly failing, and whether anything is set to start with your machine that should not be.

Backup state

Whether your files are actually backed up anywhere, tested rather than assumed. If the answer is no, the report says so plainly and what it would take to fix it.

How it works

Request the check below. We send you our support app with simple instructions, it gathers the machine's health readings, and we review them by hand. You get the report by email within one business day. If everything is healthy, the report says that and we part friends. If something needs attention, the report names it and what a care plan would cover. No charge, no obligation, and the report is yours to keep.
