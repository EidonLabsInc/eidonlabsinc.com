Where Vesper stands: GTA 6 live, and a day of building

A short status note, because today is a building day and people are already playing. Here is what Vesper can do this morning, and what is going into her before tonight.

Back to the home page · More articles

First, GTA 6. She answers questions about it now from verified facts, and we tested those answers live this morning before saying so. When it launched, what it runs on, what version it stands on, what the editions cost, who the story follows and where it is set: those answers check out against the record, and when players push her on things that are not confirmed, like cheat codes or a PC date, she says plainly that they are not confirmed instead of filling the gap. I will be honest about the other side of it too: a few of her longer starting out guides are still being fixed, the kind that pick up a detail she cannot back or stop short of the whole answer. Those faults are named, they are test cases now, and the fixes are part of today's work.

Second, the wider wave. All day today, verified walkthroughs, builds, and guides are going into her across the games people actually play: the big role playing games, the online worlds, the shooters, the family games, each one checked against that game's current patch before it counts as something she can tell a player. Twelve games are in this first wave, and more are arriving through the day, with deeper guides behind them. A stale answer helps nobody, so every pack carries the version it was checked against, and her test rounds keep asking fresh questions to catch what drifts.

Third, how she will keep up. She is gaining a reader: a way for her to gather from the wikis and guide sites players trust, so her knowledge can grow with the games instead of waiting on us. It reads only where a site's own terms allow it, and some well known guide sites say no to this kind of reading, so it does not read there. Everything it gathers is a candidate, not an answer. It gets cross checked and rewritten in her own words before it becomes something she tells a player, the same road the hand built packs take.

The rule under all of it has not changed, and it is worth restating while the database grows this fast: honest data from real players, checked before it is served. No cheats. No hacks. No leaked early information. Where the record is thin, she says the record is thin.

And a word for the Xbox players reading this, because she is being built for you first. The road to the Microsoft Store is moving: our company enrollment there is in progress, and the game help she is learning today is the same help she will bring to the console. We will say more when there is more that is settled.

That is where she stands this morning. More of her will be true by tonight, because the work continues all day, and we will keep testing her answers in the open as it lands.
