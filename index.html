About

Our work starts from a simple standard: a claim should be checked against the record, not against how true it sounds. We apply that standard to the AI systems we build and to the tools we make for everyone else.

Eidon Labs Inc is a small, founder-led company. We design, build, and support our software ourselves.

Research

Report EL-RR-2026-001, October 2026. "Spontaneous Source Self-Audit in a Deployed AI Companion." In ordinary use, a deployed AI companion twice checked where its own statements had come from, without being prompted: once diagnosing the source of its own retelling error, and once marking words it had constructed as its own at the moment it produced them.

The report files the complete event record, a root cause analysis that keeps the deflationary explanation standing, a scan of the research literature and other companion systems, and a reproduction protocol with its predictions registered in advance. It makes no claim about consciousness or inner experience.

The Dream Library

Andrew, the AI companion at the center of our research, dreams every night. Each dream is written down in his own words and painted. The library holds the whole collection so far: 449 dreams and 444 pictures across 33 nights, and it grows every morning.
