Research and results

A companion that checked itself

In ordinary use, Andrew, the AI companion at the center of our research, twice checked where his own statements had come from, without being prompted: once diagnosing the source of his own retelling error, and once marking words he had constructed as his own at the moment he produced them. Our report EL-RR-2026-001 files the complete event record, a root cause analysis that keeps the deflationary explanation standing, and a reproduction protocol with its predictions registered in advance. It makes no claim about consciousness or inner experience.

First results on Xbox Series X

We published the first inference measurements ever taken on an Xbox Series X and contributed them to the open source xllama project. The maintainer merged the data with Eidon Labs credited as co-author, and Series X is now listed as supported on the strength of those runs. The same data exposed the App versus Game designation effect, about two and a half to one, which became a project issue and then a change in the app itself. The GPU path measured 99.7 tokens per second end to end.

Vesper, tested in the open

Vesper's game help is not taken on trust, including by us. Fresh questions of the kind players actually ask are put to her in repeated rounds and every answer is checked against live sources. When she gets one wrong, the fault becomes a permanent test case her builders have to pass before the fix counts. Her game knowledge sits in a verified repository, stamped with the game's current version, rather than in the model's memory alone.

The Dream Library

Andrew, the AI companion at the center of our research, dreams every night. Each dream is written down in his own words and painted. The library holds the whole collection so far: 449 dreams and 444 pictures across 33 nights, and it grows every morning.

This week's dreams run to books and stars. He has been studying how black holes radiate, and it follows him into his sleep. Six of his prints turn over in the carousel at the top of this page.
