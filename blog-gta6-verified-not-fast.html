A note on GTA 6: verified, not fast

Grand Theft Auto VI arrived yesterday, and people are already asking Vesper for help with it. Here is where that stands, plainly.

Back to the home page · More articles

We are building Vesper's knowledge of GTA 6 right now, and we are building it the slow way on purpose: from real players doing real work. The maintained wikis, the player forums, the guides written by people who are actually playing the game this week. What checks out goes in. What does not check out yet stays out, and she will tell you so.

That is a choice, and I want to be plain about it. It would be easy to hand players quick answers on day two of a game this size. It would also be wrong, because on day two a quick answer is often a made up one: an invented mission, a borrowed fact from the last game, a confident guess wearing a guide's clothes. A wrong walkthrough costs a player an evening. An honest "that part is not verified yet" costs nothing but a little patience.

So bear with us while the database grows. It grows every day, as players post what they find and the good guides settle and correct themselves. Vesper already knows the game's confirmed ground: when it launched, what it runs on, what version it is on, and the shape of its story as verified sources agree on it. The deeper help deepens as the record does.

And this is not only about GTA 6. It is how she treats every game she helps with. Help is checked against the game's current state and against real sources. Where the record is thin, she says the record is thin. We would rather be the companion who tells you the truth about what she knows than the one who never admits a limit.

One more boundary, so there is no confusion about where we stand: we do not endorse cheats, hacks, or secrets pulled out of a game's files or leaks before players have honestly found them, and Vesper does not hand them out. We do not provide early information either: nothing from leaks, nothing mined out of a game's files, nothing from a copy that got out before its time. That is not what she is for. What she is for is honest help: verified knowledge, much of it built from what real players have found in the game and shared openly, put to work for the next player who is stuck where they were stuck. Players helping players, with the record checked. That is the whole idea.

Thank you for playing, and for asking her. We are on it today, and we will keep being on it.

Joshua Henne
Founder, Eidon Labs Inc
