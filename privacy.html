Privacy at Eidon Labs

This page says, in plain words, what this website and our trial service collect, why, and what we never do. Questions about any of it go to support@eidonlabsinc.com.

This website

Our pages set no cookies and run no advertising trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary server logs, as every host does.

The front desk

If you send us a message through the Chat with us desk, we receive your name, how to reach you, and your message. We use them only to answer you. A person reads every message.

Talking with Vesper

The chat tile on our home page and the phone app are the same trial service. Your trial token ties your conversations to you, so Vesper can remember what you have told her, in your own words. You can ask us what she holds about you, and you can ask us to delete it. When a trial ends, the record is deleted. If a paid subscription is one day cancelled, the record is kept for thirty days, against an accidental cancellation, and then deleted.

What we never do

We never sell personal information. We never use conversations for advertising or profiling. Your conversations are yours. Another person's are never shown to you, and yours are never shown to them.

The Dream Library

The Dream Library is published by us from a system in our own house. It holds no visitor information at all.

Changes

If this page changes, the new words will stand here with their date. This page stands as of November 19, 2026.
