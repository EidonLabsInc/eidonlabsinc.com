A new lab at Eidon Labs: proofs, checked by machine

Last week a mathematician working with an AI released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, checked by computer line by line. We are builders, and we recognize a tool when we see one.

Back to the home page · More articles

Last week, mathematics changed in public. A mathematician at Anthropic, working with an AI, released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, the most famous open problem in mathematics, first stated in 1859. The proof is written in Lean, a language in which a computer checks every step of the argument, and independent experts compiled it and certified that both the statement and the proof are correct. The Clay Institute's prize process will take its own time, as it should. The method is the story.

So Eidon Labs is standing up a formal verification lab: Lean and the Mathlib library, the same machinery, aimed at the open problems Paul Erdős left behind. Erdős posed more than a thousand problems. Many are still open, and some still carry the cash prizes he attached to them as his own gauge of difficulty.

The house rule for the lab is the house rule everywhere here. The model proposes. The proof checker decides. Nothing is claimed without a machine checked receipt. A result from this lab will either compile completely, with no gaps and no shortcuts, or it will not be announced at all.

To be plain about scope: this is another project, not a change of direction. Project Vesper remains our main build, unchanged and on schedule, and our managed IT services keep running for the homes and businesses who rely on them. The proof lab runs alongside, in the hours where curiosity has always done its work here.

We are grading candidate problems now, weighing difficulty, prize, and how much of each statement is already formalized. When we choose our first target, we will name it here. After that the work goes quiet, until there is something checked to show.

The lab now has a public home: the proof lab repository on GitHub. Our first target is named there, and each verified rung will be published with its receipts.
